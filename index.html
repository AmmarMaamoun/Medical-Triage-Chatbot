Why this chatbot exists

There are more people in need of mental health support than available therapy sessions. Clinical resources are limited, and many individuals wait too long before receiving attention.

This chatbot supports early mental health triage by asking five structured questions and classifying responses into severity levels, allowing attention to be prioritized.

The goal is not diagnosis and not treatment. The goal is signal detection — identifying who may need immediate support, who should be followed up, and who is currently stable.

The design is informed by established clinical frameworks including DSM-5 and PHQ-7-style screening logic, while remaining transparent and non-intrusive.

This tool does not replace clinicians. If you are in immediate danger, please contact local emergency services.
